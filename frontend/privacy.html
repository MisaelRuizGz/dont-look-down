Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your username and a hashed version of your password — we never store your password in plain text. We also store your typing results: words per minute, category, timer length, and the date of each attempt.

Guest use

Without an account, your results stay only in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to our servers and are lost if you clear your browser data or switch devices.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use tracking cookies. Local storage keeps you signed in and, for guests, remembers recent attempts on this device.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run the site's features. We don't sell or share it with third parties.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, contact us at dontlookdown.website@gmail.com Guests can clear their history at any time by clearing their browser's local storage.

Contact

Questions can be sent to dontlookdown.website@gmail.com.
